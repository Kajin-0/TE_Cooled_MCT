## Independent screening calculation — old route only

The current builder's source-level direction-aware estimate for the **rejected original route** is R_Cu = 0.296705873 ohm, NOT a measured or 3D-FEM-qualified electrical resistance. It implies I²R = 47.47 mW at 0.4 A and 296.71 mW at 1.0 A. Those are screening quantities, not known Joule destination fractions, peak current density, performance corrections or transferable values for a proposed new trace/tab/foil design. At high current the omitted electrical/thermal interconnect design could be material to the claimed cooling margin.

The independent source drawings materially constrain what may be asserted. TEC Microsystems 3MC04-044-05 (2019), PDF p.1–2, documents 6.4 mm bottom and 2.4 mm top ceramics, 3.8 mm assembly, and performance at specified hot-side/ambient media; it does **not** publish the frozen candidate's internal route dimensions. The same manufacturer's 3MC04-076-05 (2019), p.4, labels distinct **interstage connectors** and **internal metallization junctions**, showing why the generic category is real while the particular long copper-post topology is not source-qualified. The VIGO 3TE TO-66 Rev9 drawing, p.1, explicitly provides the required nine-pin functions and geometry. RMT's separate 3MC04 series adds an independent manufacturer analogue, not proof of identical grade or routing.

Source links and criteria: [the independent audit protocol](PHYSICAL_PLAUSIBILITY_AUDIT_PROTOCOL.md). Neither photographs nor illustration-only drawings establish the nominal width, alloy or mechanical rating of a particular candidate connector. That unknown must remain UNKNOWN.
