The preceding feasibility discussion records the earlier coordinate-only status. A separately constructed 0.60 mm-leg \emph{custom} assembly now moves the four ceramic planes, rebuilds the 88 legs and solder joints, shifts the metallization and detector stack, reforms the three copper returns, and raises the two detector-wire paths while keeping their pin endpoints fixed. The source 0.50 mm assembly remains unchanged. The 4.10 mm stack is a design difference from the cited manufacturer's nominal envelope, not a physical impossibility or an assertion of that manufacturer's construction.
