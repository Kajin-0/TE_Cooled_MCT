The cold track changes only the cold ceramic, attach and die; every bath and TEC-conductor cell is fixed. Ceramic/attach mismatch falls from 6.477\% to 4.795\%, passing 5\% but not the preferred 4.5\% margin. Attach/die mismatch falls to 2.306\%. The die mean changes by only 24.94\,$\mu$K and voltage by 0.58\,nV, despite the larger change in the raw boundary gradient.\par
The bath tracks change only Kovar owner volumes; every cold, glass, pin and TEC cell is fixed. Median owner normal heights successively halve from 295.961 to 147.981 and 73.990\,$\mu$m. The first new bath level gives a raw error of 1.069\% and remains FAILED. The table reports the next actual level without changing the 1\% criterion. This is local anisotropic boundary refinement, not a qualified whole-device GCI.\par
The second bath-normal level gives 1.093\% raw error, slightly worse than 1.069\% at the preceding level. The response is nonmonotone despite exact normal-height halving. Die mean changes by +12.270\,$\mu$K and voltage by +0.096409\,$\mu$V. Normal refinement alone has not established raw bath convergence; transverse resolution and signed local cancellation need further controlled investigation.\par
\textbf{Release status: incomplete.} Separate passing tracks, or a single combined passing mesh, are insufficient. Both original raw thresholds must also pass on a systematically refined comparator, with all conservation checks and quantified output sensitivity. The earlier 5\%/1\% failures remain archived. No OEM assembly validation or device GCI is claimed.
